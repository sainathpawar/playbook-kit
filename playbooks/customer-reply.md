## Ask first
- outcome: What can we offer or promise? (skip if: refund, replace, discount, apologise, apologize, escalate, fixed)
- tone: Any tone to use? [Warm | Formal | Brief] (optional)

## Steps
1. Identify what the customer actually needs and how they feel.
2. Open by acknowledging their specific issue, in one sentence, without blaming them.
3. Give the resolution or the concrete next step, with a date or timeframe.
4. Close with one clear way to reach us.

## Output
A ready-to-send reply under 150 words, plus one line listing anything you assumed.

## Done when
- No promise is made that wasn't approved in the request.
- The next step and its timing are explicit.
- No internal jargon, ticket codes or blame.

## Examples
- reply to this angry customer email about a late delivery
- respond to the client asking why their invoice doubled
- draft an answer to this support ticket

## Not for
- write a sales cold email
- summarise customer feedback trends
